# STELLAR DRIFT — Roadmap (Phase 2)

Phase 1 (see `ROADMAP.md`) is complete: the core dodge/shoot/collect loop,
four zones with bosses and bonus stages, power-ups, beeper + 128K AY sound,
parallax + banded backdrops, scrolling cave terrain, high-score table,
selectable ships, IM2 timing and pre-shifted sprites.

This phase added **depth, feel and content**. **Every item below is now
implemented and headless-tested** (`.github/workflows/game.yml` asserts the
title, the first zone, a long crash-free run, a terrain-crash GAME OVER, and
a 128K AY capture). Status: ✅ done.

> The backdrop stays **black** by design (readability, low eye-strain):
> colour comes from sprites, stars and the cave walls — never large bright
> paper blocks.

---

## 1. Make the cave matter

- ✅ **Terrain collision** — the cave is a per-column heightmap
  (`ceil_h`/`floor_h`); flying the ship into a wall crashes it
  (`cave_collide` → `ship_hit`).
- ✅ **Narrowing passages & caverns** — the heightmap scrolls via a bounded
  random walk whose amplitude grows with the zone, giving pinch points and
  open caverns instead of a flat band.
- ✅ **Smoother scrolling** — the 3-layer parallax starfield is pixel-smooth
  and the cave heightmap now advances every 2 frames (repainted each frame).

## 2. Combat depth

- ✅ **More enemy types** — diver (swoops at the ship), mine (drifts),
  drone (homes + fires), turret (fixed, aimed fire), each with its own
  sprite and AI in the object loop.
- ✅ **Multi-phase bosses** — the boss enrages below half HP (twice the fire
  rate, turns red) and has a distinct silhouette per zone (`boss_tab`).
- ✅ **Smart-bomb** — `B` detonates every on-screen hazard for points
  (limited count shown in the HUD).
- ✅ **Chargeable shot** — holding Fire builds a charge that releases a
  magenta piercing bolt (extra boss damage).

## 3. Scoring & replayability

- ✅ **Combo / multiplier** — chained kills raise a HUD multiplier
  (`x2`…`x6`); a hit resets it.
- ✅ **Graze bonus** — near-misses with enemy fire score a rate-limited
  bonus (`GRZ`).
- ✅ **Difficulty modes** — Cadet / Pilot / Ace on the title, scaling
  starting lives (and feeding the wave density curve).
- ✅ **Authored, varied waves per zone** — six per-zone `wave*` scripts
  introducing each enemy type and escalating.

## 4. Feel & animation

- ✅ **Ship banking** — climb/dive sheared frames (generated by mksprites),
  selected from the vertical intent.
- ✅ **Real exhaust flame** — a 2-frame animated thruster behind the ship.
- ✅ **Gentler invuln tell** — pulses the ship colour (cyan↔white) instead
  of hiding it (option `opt_softblink`).
- ✅ **Debris & sparks** — pixel sparks scatter from kills (`do_debris`).
- ✅ **Drifting background detail** — a dim distant planet sprite drifts in
  the far background (dropped automatically under frame-budget pressure).

## 5. Audio

- ✅ **Per-zone AY themes** — six melodies selected per zone (`music_tab`).
- ✅ **AY sound effects on 128K** — shoot/pickup/hit blip on PSG channel B
  alongside the beeper (harmless on 48K).
- ✅ **Music on/off** toggle on the title.

## 6. Content & presentation

- ✅ **More zones** — six named zones (Orion Drift, Crimson Veil, Sapphire
  Expanse, Magenta Storm, Emerald Rift, Void Nexus) cycling with rising
  difficulty.
- ✅ **Mid-bosses** — a multi-hit "warship" appears in the later zone waves.
- ✅ **Intro & inter-zone text** — a centred zone-name banner on entry.
- ✅ **Demo / attract mode** — after ~15s idle the title hands off to an AI
  pilot with a DEMO banner; fire returns to the title.

## 7. Technical & persistence

- ✅ **Save the high-score table to tape** — ROM SA-BYTES/LD-BYTES via `S`/`L`
  on the title (works on tape machines incl. the HC-2000).
- ✅ **128K RAM banking** — detected at boot, the high-score table is backed
  into a spare RAM bank (`0x7FFD` paging); a 48K-safe no-op otherwise.
- ✅ **Frame-rate guard** — each frame is measured against the 50 Hz tick;
  the background planet is dropped when a frame overruns to hold the pace.
- ✅ **Gameplay regression tests** in CI — scripted inputs assert the title,
  the first zone, a crash-free long run, and a terrain-crash GAME OVER.

## 8. Accessibility & options

- ✅ **Remappable keys** — a third "custom" control scheme with a
  define-keys flow (`D` on the title, then the five keys).
- ✅ **Slow / practice mode** — `6` on the title: no life lost on a hit and
  the game runs at half speed for practice.
- ✅ **Photosensitivity option** — `5` on the title softens the
  screen-shake / border-flash.
